# v0.4.0 — Manual landmark registration

Place landmark pairs between two tissue images and inspect the registration result, in a new Manual registration tab that works on MerXen manual-registration stores.

## Manual registration

- A new **Manual registration** tab and a **Manual Registration ▸ Load image data** section in the Dataset loader open a MerXen `<id>_prepared.zarr` (to place landmarks) or `<run_id>_registered.zarr` (to inspect a result).
- Draw crops on each image to exclude tile-seam borders and coverslip artefacts.
- Coarse alignment with large rotations and flips: a 180° button, Flip H / Flip V, rotation in steps from ±90° down to ±0.1°, and shifts in selectable steps.
- Flicker comparison between the two images (F, hold Shift+F to peek) and auto-switching after each click.
- Landmark pairs with live fit and leave-one-out residuals, warnings, and held-out (disabled) pairs.
- Draft and finish saving with conflict detection, a history of previous payloads and local drafts that are offered after an interrupted session.
- A read-only result view with the registered image, landmark overlays, a residual table and a banner with the preset, refinement and flow status.
- `--registration-store <path>` opens a store at startup.

## Changes

- The Manual registration tab is appended after Zarr metadata; the existing tab order is unchanged.
- Recently viewed remembers registration stores.
- The standalone loaders open a registration store in the Manual registration tab instead of loading it as a standalone dataset.
- Image-only stores without transcripts skip the transcript autoload.
- Display rotation is set to 0° and disabled in a registration session; registration layers are never rotated by it.
- napari is pinned below 0.8.

## Requirements

Stores written by a MerXen version with the manual-registration workflows, schema version 1. The viewer refuses newer schema versions. See [docs/manual_registration_guide.md](../manual_registration_guide.md).
