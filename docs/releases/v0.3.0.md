# v0.3.0 — Paired spatial viewing and linked UMAPs

Compare aligned MERSCOPE and Xenium datasets in one Napari window with shared controls, linked cell inspection, and spatial/UMAP region selection.

## Paired viewing

- Open a pair in **side-by-side** mode with MERSCOPE on the left and Xenium on the right, linked navigation, paired layer visibility, and shared image, segmentation, gene, and cell-type controls.
- Use **stacked overlay** mode to display both platforms in their common coordinate system, with independent visibility for platform-qualified layers.
- Switch between layouts without reopening stores or rebuilding layers. Independent stacked-overlay visibility settings are preserved.
- Inspect cells and transcripts at the same aligned coordinate in both datasets.
- Launch paired sessions from the Dataset loader or with `--paired-view side-by-side` / `--paired-view stacked-overlay` and both dataset paths.

## UMAPs, regions, and cell types

- View linked spatial and UMAP selections, including separate MERSCOPE and Xenium UMAPs with consistent cell-type colours across platforms.
- Draw regions in spatial views or UMAPs to highlight selected cells and show cell-type counts and percentages. Spatial regions apply to both aligned stores; a UMAP region applies to its own platform.
- Support MerXen cell-type hierarchies and hybrid masks, including discovery of named map-first annotation tables.
- Add shared dataset rotation controls and marker-reference catalog tooling with panel-matched reference support.

## Fixes and compatibility

- Restore cell-type gene ordering across derived stores, render cell-type masks on first selection, and prevent stale cell-type ID joins.
- Align the storage stack with MerXen: SpatialData 0.8, Zarr 3, and AnnData 0.12 (below 0.13).

## Paired-data requirements

Paired viewing requires matching, complete **MerXen v2 materialized stores**: a MERSCOPE `merxen_alignment` manifest and the corresponding Xenium `merxen_alignment_pair_reference`. Paired mode validates the pair and consumes it read-only; it does not perform registration or repair missing artifacts. Re-materialize incomplete, stale, or mismatched pairs upstream in MerXen.

Per-cell statistic overlays and tissue-annotation creation/export remain unavailable while a pair is active. Each platform's UMAP comes from its own store; the embeddings are not harmonised.
